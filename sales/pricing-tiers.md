# Vete Pricing Tiers

_Last updated: 2026-02-07 by Nyx 🌙_

## Overview

Three tiers designed for the Paraguayan market with PPP-adjusted pricing.

---

## 🥉 Plan Básico

**Target:** Solo practitioners, small clinics (1-2 vets)

### Price
- **PYG 150,000/mes** (~$20 USD)
- Annual: PYG 1,500,000/año (2 meses gratis)

### Included
- ✅ Patient records (unlimited)
- ✅ Appointment scheduling
- ✅ Basic invoicing
- ✅ 1 user account
- ✅ Email support
- ✅ Mobile app access

### Limits
- 1 location
- 500 patients/month
- Basic reports only

---

## 🥈 Plan Profesional

**Target:** Growing clinics (3-5 vets)

### Price
- **PYG 350,000/mes** (~$48 USD)
- Annual: PYG 3,500,000/año (2 meses gratis)

### Included
Everything in Básico, plus:
- ✅ Up to 5 user accounts
- ✅ Inventory management
- ✅ Prescription management
- ✅ WhatsApp reminders
- ✅ Advanced reports & analytics
- ✅ Multi-vet scheduling
- ✅ Priority email + chat support

### Limits
- 1 location
- 2,000 patients/month
- 5 WhatsApp reminders/day

---

## 🥇 Plan Clínica

**Target:** Large clinics, hospitals (6+ vets)

### Price
- **PYG 650,000/mes** (~$89 USD)
- Annual: PYG 6,500,000/año (2 meses gratis)

### Included
Everything in Profesional, plus:
- ✅ Unlimited users
- ✅ Multi-location support
- ✅ Laboratory integration
- ✅ Hospitalization module
- ✅ Surgery scheduling
- ✅ Staff management & payroll
- ✅ Custom reports
- ✅ API access
- ✅ Dedicated account manager
- ✅ Phone support
- ✅ On-site training (1 session)

### Limits
- Up to 5 locations
- Unlimited patients
- Unlimited WhatsApp
