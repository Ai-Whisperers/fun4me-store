## Pricing Models in the Industry

### Per-Location Pricing
- Most common model
- Base fee + per-vet or per-user add-ons
- Example: $199/month base + $49/additional user

### Tiered Feature Pricing
- Basic → Pro → Enterprise
- Features unlock at higher tiers
- Example: Basic ($99), Pro ($199), Enterprise (custom)

### Transaction-Based
- Lower base + % of transactions
- Common for payment processing features
- Example: $49/month + 2.9% on payments

## LatAm/Paraguay Considerations

### Purchasing Power Parity
- Paraguay GDP per capita: ~$5,800 USD
- USA GDP per capita: ~$76,000 USD
- **PPP factor: ~0.08** (8% of US pricing)

## Key Differentiators for Vete

1. **Local language & support** - Spanish (Paraguay)
2. **Local payment methods** - Bancard, QR payments
3. **WhatsApp integration** - Critical for PY market
4. **Offline capability** - Internet reliability issues
5. **Simple UI** - Many vets aren't tech-savvy
