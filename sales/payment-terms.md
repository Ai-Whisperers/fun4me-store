# Vete Payment Terms & Conditions

_Effective: February 2026_

---

## 1. Subscription Billing

### 1.1 Billing Cycle
- **Monthly plans**: Billed on the same day each month (e.g., signup on Feb 15 = billed every 15th)
- **Annual plans**: Billed once per year on signup anniversary
- Invoices sent via email 3 days before charge

### 1.2 Payment Due Date
- Payment due on billing date
- **Grace period**: 5 days after due date
- Service suspension after grace period if unpaid

### 1.3 Proration
- Mid-cycle upgrades: prorated charge for remaining days
- Mid-cycle downgrades: credit applied to next billing cycle
- Add-ons: prorated from activation date

---

## 2. Payment Methods

### 2.1 Accepted Methods
| Method | Processing Time | Fees |
|--------|----------------|------|
| Bancard (credit/debit) | Instant | None |
| Bank transfer | 1-2 business days | None |
| QR payments (Tigo/Personal) | Instant | None |
| Cash (in-office) | Instant | None |

### 2.2 Automatic Payments
- Card payments are automatic on billing date
- Bank transfers require manual payment
- Failed auto-payments: 3 retry attempts over 5 days

### 2.3 Currency
- All invoices in **Paraguayan Guaraníes (PYG)**
- USD conversion available on request (rate at time of invoice)

---

## 3. Late Payments

### 3.1 Grace Period
- **5 business days** after due date
- Full access maintained during grace period
- Reminder sent on day 1, 3, and 5

### 3.2 After Grace Period
- **Day 6-15**: Account in "limited mode" (read-only access)
- **Day 16-30**: Account suspended (no access, data preserved)
- **Day 31+**: Account eligible for deletion (30-day notice given)

### 3.3 Late Fees
- No late fees for first 30 days
- After 30 days: 2% monthly interest on unpaid balance
- Collections referral after 90 days

### 3.4 Reactivation
- Full payment required to restore access
- Data preserved for 90 days after suspension
- Reactivation within 24 hours of payment

---

## 4. Refunds

### 4.1 Money-Back Guarantee
- **30-day** full refund on first payment
- No questions asked
- Refund processed within 5 business days

### 4.2 Annual Plan Refunds
- Full refund within first 30 days
- After 30 days: prorated refund minus 2 months (discount recovery)
- No refund after 6 months

### 4.3 Non-Refundable
- One-time services (migration, training, setup)
- SMS/messaging credits already used
- Add-ons after first billing cycle

### 4.4 Refund Process
1. Request via email to soporte@vete.com.py
2. Confirmation within 24 hours
3. Refund processed within 5 business days
4. Same payment method as original charge

---

## 5. Cancellation

### 5.1 How to Cancel
- Email: soporte@vete.com.py
- In-app: Settings → Billing → Cancel Subscription
- WhatsApp: +595 981 324 569

### 5.2 Cancellation Effective Date
- End of current billing period
- No partial month refunds for monthly plans
- Access continues until period ends

### 5.3 Data After Cancellation
- **30 days**: Full data export available
- **60 days**: Read-only access to export data
- **90 days**: Data permanently deleted

### 5.4 Resubscription
- Can resubscribe anytime within 90 days
- Data restored if within retention period
- New pricing may apply

---

## 6. Price Changes

### 6.1 Notice Period
- **30 days** written notice for price increases
- Notice via email and in-app notification

### 6.2 Grandfathering
- Existing customers keep current price for 12 months
- After 12 months, new pricing applies
- Annual plans locked at signup price for plan duration

### 6.3 Price Decreases
- Applied immediately on next billing cycle
- No action required from customer

---

## 7. Disputes

### 7.1 Billing Disputes
- Report within 30 days of charge
- Email soporte@vete.com.py with invoice number
- Resolution within 5 business days

### 7.2 Chargebacks
- Contact us first before disputing with bank
- Chargebacks may result in account suspension
- Resolved chargebacks restore account access

- No payment required to start

- Automatic downgrade to free tier after trial

### 8.2 Beta/Early Adopter
- Discounted pricing locked for 12 months
- May include features still in development
- Feedback participation encouraged

### 8.3 Non-Profits
- 30% discount requires verification
- Annual renewal of non-profit status
